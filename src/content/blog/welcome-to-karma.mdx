## Write without friction

Karma keeps your words in plain files. You can make text **bold**, *italic*, or <u>underlined</u>, and drop a [link to the docs](https://docs.astro.build) when you need one.

### A few things to try

- Format a sentence and save.
- Reopen it — the formatting is still there.
- Publish and rebuild the site.
